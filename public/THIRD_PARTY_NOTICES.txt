## Elevation data

`src/assets/bulgaria-dem.json` is derived from NASA/USGS Shuttle Radar
Topography Mission (SRTM) data, which is in the public domain. The values were
retrieved through the Open Topo Data SRTM 90m API. Open Topo Data is the
retrieval service and is not represented as the owner of the underlying SRTM
elevation data.
